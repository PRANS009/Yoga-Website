04 / KARMA YOGA PHILOSOPHY

Let your practice
become an act of care.

Karma Yoga is the path of selfless action: do your work with care, serve others, and loosen the expectation of personal reward.

Read more · Sivananda Yoga India ↗
01

Nishkama Karma

Give your best effort without making praise or reward the purpose of your action.

02

Seva · selfless service

Offer time, attention or help because it is useful to someone else.

03

Care in daily duty

Bring honesty and attention to study, family responsibilities and everyday work.

04

Steadiness in outcomes

Reflect on what you can improve while avoiding dependence on success or recognition.

A SMALL PRACTICE FOR TODAY

Help a classmate understand a topic, care for a shared space, or offer your time to a community task. Afterwards, reflect: “Was I focused on helping, or on being noticed?”
